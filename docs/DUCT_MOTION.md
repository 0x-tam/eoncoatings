# Retained AC duct motion

The hero trial has been removed. The homepage keeps its original MaterialExplorer layout, cards, photographs and camera transitions.

The photographic mold reveal and drifting dust are retained, with condensation beads and slow wall drips, in `components/campaign/duct-motion.ts`, with two assets under `public/images/duct-motion/`. The effect draws inside the original registered duct image and grille clip. Mold is clipped to solid duct metal, excluding the open right branch. The build-up clock starts when the camera reaches the duct interior. Its age persists while switching care views; dust and moisture continue slowly while AC care is open. Motion pauses when the document is hidden and respects reduced motion.

Validation: TypeScript passed. No trial components, styles, films or generated room runtime remain in the project. The discarded experiment was moved to a recoverable folder outside the project.
