# Eon Coatings: backlink plan

Focus: earn relevant links that send villa owners, property managers and facilities teams to useful pages. Start after the redesign is live on eoncoatings.com. These are proposed actions; no outreach has been sent and no listings have been purchased.

## First 30 days

1. **Existing relationships first.** Ask Eon for its supplier, installer, property-management and trade-association contacts. Where there is a genuine relationship, request a listing on the partner's supplier or service-provider page. Link residential partners to `/services/home-villa-ac-duct-cleaning/`; coating partners to the relevant coating service. Do not claim accreditation or partnership without confirmation.
2. **Recover existing mentions.** Search for Eon Coatings, its old site URLs and project names. Ask publishers to turn an existing mention into a link or update a broken destination. Keep historical article URLs stable; the few changed service/directory URLs have permanent redirects.
3. **Check owned business listings.** Confirm the website, phone and Mussafah address in Google Business Profile and the company's social accounts. These help customers find the right business; don't treat social links as guaranteed ranking gains. [Google's local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en).
4. **Prepare one approved project story.** Use real work, real photographs and client-approved scope/results. Explain the surface/system, preparation, work and aftercare. Invite the client to reference that case study where it serves their readers.

## Specific opportunities to validate with Eon

- **The Nano Company:** Eon's LinkedIn activity identifies a relationship with the group. Ask Eon to confirm current ownership/relationship and the appropriate corporate website contact, then propose a factual company-profile link.
- **Innovo:** Eon's LinkedIn contains a published office-treatment account. Ask the account owner whether Innovo would approve a joint case study and link from a project/news page. Do not infer permission from the social post.
- **Suppliers and certification partners:** Ask Eon for the actual issuing bodies and current supplier agreements. Only request a certified-applicator listing if Eon qualifies; similarly named “HealthGuard” brands are not interchangeable.
- **Abu Dhabi villa-management and interiors firms:** Identify 10 real businesses serving the same audience. Offer a practical maintenance guide relevant to their clients, not a reciprocal-link bargain.

The first two leads come from [Eon's public LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eon-coatings/); verify relationship status before contact.

## Days 30–90

Publish two useful resources: a villa AC-access checklist and a surface/fabric aftercare guide. Get Eon's technical team to review them. Offer these to relevant property managers, interiors businesses and facilities publications when they answer a real reader question. Pitch one documented case study to a UAE property/facilities editor. Any specific publication, eligibility, cost and editorial terms should be checked before committing.

Aim for 5 carefully researched contacts per week and one follow-up after 7–10 business days. A planning target is 3–5 useful placements over 90 days, not a guarantee. Prefer editorial fit and qualified referral enquiries over raw link counts.

## Tracking and boundaries

Maintain: business/publication, relevant page, relationship, contact, proposed resource, target URL, first contact, follow-up, outcome, live referring URL, referral enquiries. Review monthly with the marketing team. Use GA4 referral traffic and Search Console's Links report once configured; compare qualified enquiries, not just rankings.

Use ordinary brand/resource names as link text. Avoid bulk directories, automated comments, paid ranking links, mandatory keyword anchors and link exchanges. Genuine paid sponsorships must be appropriately identified and link-qualified. [Google's link-spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam).
