# Authentic HGPP display materials

The website uses the original badge, shield plaque artwork, and flat institution certificate example downloaded unchanged from the published old site. No generated replacement QR codes or certification text are used in these placements.

- Badge: https://eoncoatings.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Multi-surface-sticker.png
- Plaque: https://eoncoatings.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/healthguard-protection.jpg
- Certificate: https://eoncoatings.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/healthguard-institute.jpg

These are the original web assets, not print-production masters. QR patterns are preserved byte-for-byte; their scan destinations have not been independently verified. The plaque is the published product artwork, not a newly generated product rendering. Rejected generated display materials are archived outside public assets.

Badge and plaque source files remain untouched and are positioned over separate generated wall/holder backgrounds in the webpage.
