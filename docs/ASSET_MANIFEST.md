# Campaign assets

Current campaign assets, prompts, generated masters, exact crops and native dimensions are in assets/campaign/asset-manifest.json. The active mobile image is portrait v2. Web derivatives used by the site are in public/images/campaign. All source variants remain in assets/campaign.

The seven services use distinct appropriate supporting imagery: ventilation, metal touchpoint, fabric crop, real veined marble, carpet/upholstery, mattress construction and exterior materials. Hero stone/fabric details are literal source crops. The hero porous stone is not represented as marble.

Original EON logo and company-published HGPP certificates remain in public/images and public/images/authentic. Article photography from EON remains associated with its source articles. Generated campaign imagery is illustrative and is not a recorded EON project, test or treatment result.

Native output is 1536x1024 for landscape and 1024x1536 for portrait. The higher native landscape request was not fulfilled by the available generator. No upscaled export is called native.
