# Life, well kept.

Creative reset, 11 September 2026. This document replaces previous duct and photographic-wipe directions.

## Campaign choice

Three original still-life studies were generated before layout. Study 03 was selected after full-size inspection: complete chair construction, substantial stone, restrained props and the strongest upper-left negative space. Study 01 added unwanted books/bowl and crowded the cup against the right edge. Study 02 cropped the chair too severely and flattened the material hierarchy.

The splash in all three studies looked spoon-like. It was removed from the selected master. No simulated coating barrier or outcome is represented. The campaign is illustrative, not EON project proof.

The landscape master is 1536 by 1024 pixels. A 3072 by 2048 native output was requested, but the available image tool returned 1536 by 1024. No upscaling is presented as native detail. Close views have this resolution limit.

## Type and composition

Fraunces variable, upright, weight 450 with restrained softness, paired with Source Sans 3 for readable service text. Both are SIL Open Font License 1.1. The selected headline was inspected in the actual 1440 by 1000 browser composition before the wider site was finished. Font files are locally hosted and compressed to WOFF2. The original licensed fonts and license texts remain in public/fonts.

Petrol ink #163C3F and deeper #08292D hold the image and navigation. Mineral paper #EEEBE4 supports reading. Terracotta appears principally in photography. Sections alternate image, substantial editorial type, dense numbered services, a dark architectural AC story and quiet company information. No seven-card template, 3D scene, slider, particles or scroll hijacking.

## Interaction storyboard

1. Overview: real EON mark, headline, service descriptor, enquiry and material navigation. Ordinary services and contact links remain available independently of motion.
2. Hover or keyboard focus: a small marker label indicates the corresponding material. No automatic zoom or extra image load.
3. Stone commit: scale and translate the same photograph into its stone surface. Reveal relevant marble and shared-surface service links.
4. Fabric commit: move the same source image into the woven seat. Reveal protection, deep cleaning and mattress routes.
5. Air commit: explicitly cut to the separate ventilation photograph after decode. The service panel works even if the optional air photograph cannot load.
6. Return: restore the exact overview and focus the initiating text material control.
7. Continue: normal document scroll into full service discovery, company information, FAQ, journal and enquiry.

Hotspots use normalized source coordinates and the actual rendered image dimensions, including the portrait source. Zoom translation is bounded by raster edges. Repeated selections update one state with interruptible CSS transitions. Reduced motion disables animation and preserves every destination. The page does not pin or trap scrolling.
