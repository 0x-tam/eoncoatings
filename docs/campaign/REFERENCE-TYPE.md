## Recommended licensed pair

Use Fraunces variable for display and Source Sans 3 for reading and controls. Recommendation: Fraunces 500, optical size 96, SOFT 15, WONK 0; use upright type first. The shaped serifs and rounded details give the material campaign character without relying on ultra-condensed display lettering. Source Sans 3 supplies a restrained, readable supporting voice.

Start the actual hero with two deliberate lines: "Life," then "well kept." Try 104 to 132px on a 1440px view, line-height .98 and letter-spacing -.035em. Begin mobile around 52 to 64px, then choose breaks against the portrait asset at 390 and 360px. Do not force the desktop setting onto mobile. Body starts at 17 to 18px with 1.5 to 1.6 line-height; navigation 15 to 16px, normal tracking. These are test values, not a final browser-verified specimen.

Both families are available under SIL Open Font License 1.1: [Fraunces license](https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces/blob/master/OFL.txt), [Source Sans license](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans/blob/release/LICENSE.md). Official Google Fonts TTF files and their license texts are saved in ./fonts. Retain licenses when distributing. WOFF2 conversion was not completed because fontTools is unavailable in the default Python runtime.
