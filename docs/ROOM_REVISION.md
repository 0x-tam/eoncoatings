# Bright room and AC priority revision

The user's follow-up replaces the still-life hero with a bright living room, visible ducted AC outlet, L-shaped blue sofa and travertine table. Cobalt buttons/panels and citron accents now carry across the site. AC cleaning is first in hero messaging, booking links, service navigation and homepage service list. The dedicated AC feature follows immediately after the hero. All seven service categories and existing article routes remain.

All five new campaign images were generated and inspected before final validation. Main room, duct and stone masters are1672x941; fabric and illustrative microscopy are1536x1024. They are not represented as4K. Separate close photographs retain detail at the final inspection size. The camera first magnifies the room source, then dissolves to its reference-matched detail. This is a photographic transition, not a seamless optical zoom or a3D scene. Stone texture and blue upholstery are matched visually, not pixel-identical regenerated crops.

Fabric includes an expandable scientific-style microscopy photograph explicitly marked illustrative. It does not diagnose organisms in the pictured room. Stone shows everyday residue. The duct shows accumulated dust, not a claimed customer case or treatment result.

Desktop uses the architectural room behind the headline. Mobile uses the same room source at a tighter crop with text above, so every object remains recognizable. On selection, the mobile photograph comes directly after the title and before service information. All controls remain accessible without hover. Back restores the initiating hotspot through a stable ref key. Reduced-motion styles disable transforms/dissolves; loading failures leave service information usable.

Generation prompts and native masters: assets/room. Browser captures and independent review: evidence/room. The previous public publication remains unchanged; the earlier source-push authorization was not granted by this design follow-up.
