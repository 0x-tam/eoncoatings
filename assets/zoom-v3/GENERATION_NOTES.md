# Photographic detail assets

The closed grille was generated as a matching high-resolution edit of the existing calibrated aperture photograph. Its native dimensions are2139x735. It is initially registered at room coordinates976,44,303,103, then calibrated to976,43,303,103.823 with a vertical shear of-4.66 across its width. The earlier room-wide closed photograph remains the base; the high-resolution grille is a permanent detail tile from overview onward.

A deep-aperture variant was also generated and preserved as duct-open.png, but the camera implementation uses the established deep inspection photograph behind a specific grille slot instead. No transition changes the whole visible image to this variant.

Measured dark slot intervals in the native closed grille:
- x300:y441 through459
- x1070:y329 through348
- x1800:y222 through243

These points define the opening used by the camera to travel between two photographed slats. The slats do not move or disappear independently.
