# Bright room campaign

Built-in image_gen was used for all five images. Native outputs were preserved, then encoded as WebP quality94; the overview also has AVIF quality90. No upscale or 3D renderer was used. The generator returned1672x941 for room/duct/stone and1536x1024 for fabric/microscopy, despite higher requested dimensions. These are separate reference-matched photographs, not a single continuous optical capture. The transition magnifies the original room, then dissolves into the newly generated detail.

## Fabric and microscopy
